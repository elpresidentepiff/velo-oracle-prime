## RUNNER NOTES (from Spotlight)

**It's Tim:**  
Four-time Flat winner in Britain for George Boughey; showing his best form on AW and quick ground on turf; in rear in a 1m3f handicap at Down Royal last September on Irish debut; holds a Supreme Novices' entry; heavy ground is unlikely to suit.

**Mossy Way:**  
Showed ability in bumpers last season for Pat Doyle; started this season well with an emphatic bumper win at Limerick in November on heavy; well held in winners' company in his two starts for this stable (last of seven in Grade 2 at DRF last time); makes hurdles debut.

**Boedic:**  
Shaped with promise on debut in Fairyhouse bumper last April; disappointing odds-on favourite on return in Thurles maiden hurdle, fading tamely into fifth behind the useful Zeus Power; made a costly mistake two out when third behind a shock winner at Down Royal last month.

**Court Compliance (NAP):**  
Beaten 2l by today's rival Newbrook Diamond in a point at Oldtown last March; good standard of form in taking the runner-up spot on all three maiden hurdle outings, the first of which was over 2m6f at this venue, beaten 1/2l by Champagne Kid, subsequent winner of a valuable handicap at Leopardstown; solid displays at Down Royal and Navan underline his chance.

**Newbrook Diamond:**  
Beat today's rival Court Compliance at Oldtown last March; useful form in maiden hurdles; slight concern he has not progressed since second at Naas on the first attempt, and that he hung badly when third over 2m at this venue December; clear second to a well-fancied rival over 2m at Naas; found little from two out over a near-2m3f trip last time.

**The Ducks Walk:**  
Ran three times in points last season; pulled up in races on either side of a win at Ballinaboola; faces a tough task.

**Thisain'Tthat:**  
Made a satisfactory debut over 2m here a fortnight ago; likely to need more experience.

**Jack's Sister:**  
Dam won over jumps as well as on the Flat, ran twice on the Flat for Johnny Feane; best watched here.
