## SP Terminology Correction
- `race_winner_sp_avg_winning_picks`: avg SP of the actual winner in races where our pick WON
- `race_winner_sp_avg_all_picks`: avg SP of the actual winner across all picks (wins + misses)
- These are NOT the pick's own SP — pick SP is rarely available in the sigma row artifacts
- Do not mix these fields.
