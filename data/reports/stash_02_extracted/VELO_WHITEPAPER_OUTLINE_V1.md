# VELO Whitepaper Outline V1

**Title:** VELO: An Auditable Racing Intelligence Operating System  
**Subtitle:** ETCSLV architecture, live evidence, and governed confidence in horse racing  
**Date:** 2026-04-29  
**Audience:** Technical investors, partners, analytics buyers, governance-minded operators

---

## Abstract

VELO is an auditable racing intelligence operating system, not a tip sheet. It generates pre-race intelligence, records what it said, reconciles against closed results, ranks which internal signals deserve trust, and exposes its own confidence to the operator. This paper explains ETCSLV as the architecture, Evidence Vault as memory, Sigma and Router as verification, Signal Stack as operator interface, and candidate lanes as governed promotion paths.
