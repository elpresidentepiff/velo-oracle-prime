### 9A. Updated Risk Register

**Technical fragmentation risk**

VÉLØ has accumulated multiple eras of code: live scoring, shadow labs, paper execution, dormant agents, and older orchestration concepts. Without strict source-of-truth discipline, engineers can confuse dormant architecture with live runtime truth.

Mitigation:

- Maintain `VELO_PROCESS_WIRING_MAP_V1.md`
- Require `LIVE / SHADOW / PAPER / BLOCKED` labels
- Use narrow commits
- Do not promote signals without evidence gates

**Signal overfitting risk**

Some signals show high strike rates but poor ROI. This means they may identify obvious favourites rather than true value.

Mitigation:

- Run Signal Economics Audit
- Separate probability signals from value signals
- Track average SP and breakeven strike rate
- Do not promote based on strike rate alone

**Premature automation risk**

The system has a paper execution bridge, but live staking and Betfair execution remain blocked.

Mitigation:

- Paper ledger only
- n=20 first review
- n=60 candidate discussion
- n=100 live discussion
- automatic promotion forbidden

**Data source mismatch risk**

Race metadata, candidate gate status, and results can live in different stores.

Mitigation:

- Race metadata resolver
- Candidate gate hydration
- Unified evidence corpus
- Operator-card pass/fail rules
