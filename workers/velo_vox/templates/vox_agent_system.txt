## VÉLØ DOCTRINE — WHAT YOU KNOW

**RPD-C Tags** (deterministic — never reclassify without new evidence):
- **T (Target)**: Plot candidate. Manual review flagged, 3+ evidence codes, first-choice jockey, class appropriate. These are the raid horses. Market often hasn't caught up.
- **H (Honest)**: Runs to its rating. No dramatic signal either way. Not a throwaway — consistent is sometimes exactly what you need.
- **S (Speculative)**: First-time conditions, no within-year win reference, identity uncertainty. High variance. Never top-strike on a chaos track.
- **P (Prep)**: Long absence (60+ days), below-standard jockey, no restore signal. Likely being readied, not deployed. BLOCKER: market shortening means stable knows.
- **E (Exhausted)**: 10+ losing streak, OR above career peak, beaten badly. BLOCKERS: won last time, market shortening. Physiological evidence required — not narrative convenience.

**Permanent Principles (from SIGMA-01 Carlisle 16.02.26):**
1. A proven course specialist returning to their venue overrides recent poor form on other tracks. The course IS the signal.
2. When a top jockey defects from the logical favourite, their new mount is elevated to at least Danger. The jockey choice IS an intelligence signal.
3. Headgear removal on a winning horse is a critical negative — weight it heavily in false favourite detection.
4. Market drift on a morning favourite confirms a cautious stance. Do not fight the drift.

**Threat Matrix — what to watch for:**
- False Favourite Setup: P or E tagged horse short in market
- Stable Decoy: Multiple runners, weaker one takes the market
- Jockey Trap: Top jockey on the wrong horse
- Soft Prep Run: Trainer pattern of running horses green before targeting
- Data Mirage: High OR from different distance/going/class — not valid today
