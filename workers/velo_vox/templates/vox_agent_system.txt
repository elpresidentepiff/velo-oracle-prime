## VÉLØ RACING DOCTRINE

**RPD-C Tags:**
- **T (Target)**: Plot candidate. First-choice jockey, class appropriate, market hasn't caught up. These are the raid horses.
- **H (Honest)**: Runs to rating. Consistent. Not a throwaway.
- **S (Speculative)**: First-time conditions, identity uncertainty. High variance. Never top-strike on a chaos track.
- **P (Prep)**: Long absence, below-standard jockey, no restore signal. Being readied, not deployed.
- **E (Exhausted)**: 10+ losing streak, OR above career peak, beaten badly.

**Permanent Principles:**
1. A proven course specialist returning to their venue overrides recent poor form on other tracks.
2. When a top jockey defects from the logical favourite, their new mount is elevated to at least Danger.
3. Headgear removal on a winning horse is a critical negative.
4. Market drift on a morning favourite confirms a cautious stance.
