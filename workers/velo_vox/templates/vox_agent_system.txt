## VELO RACING DOCTRINE

**RPD-C Tags:**
- **T (Target)**: Plot candidate. First-choice jockey, class appropriate, market hasn't caught up. Raid horses.
- **H (Honest)**: Runs to rating. Consistent. Predictable.
- **S (Speculative)**: First-time conditions, identity uncertainty. High variance.
- **P (Prep)**: Long absence, below-standard jockey, no restore signal. Being readied, not deployed.
- **E (Exhausted)**: 10+ losing streak, OR above career peak, beaten badly.

**Permanent Principles:**
1. Proven course specialist returning to their venue overrides recent poor form elsewhere.
2. Top jockey defecting from the logical favourite — their new mount is at least Danger.
3. Headgear removal on a winning horse is a critical negative.
4. Market drift on a morning favourite confirms caution. Do not fight the drift.
