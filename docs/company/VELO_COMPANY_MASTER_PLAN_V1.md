## The Thesis

Horse racing generates billions in market activity annually. The intelligence layer serving this market is largely unauditable, untraceable, and evidence-free. Existing tipster services provide picks with no methodology disclosure, no post-race reconciliation, and no honest miss analysis.

VÉLØ builds the auditable alternative. Every prediction is timestamped, every result reconciled, every signal ranked by live evidence. The evidence vault is the moat.

## Phase 2: Product Core (60–120 days)

**Goal:** Build the minimum viable product layer on top of the evidence engine.

Core modules:
1. **Daily Race Intelligence** — VP score, tier, confidence band, reason codes
2. **Confidence Bands** — HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW with historical performance attached
3. **Frame Probability** — how often does VÉLØ's pick finish in the top 3 at this confidence?
4. **Winner Probability** — VP score explained in plain language
5. **Signal Strength** — which sidecars are active on this pick
6. **Router Lane Status** — is this pick in an evidence-grade lane?
7. **Post-Race Audit** — what happened, what did VÉLØ get right/wrong
8. **Historical Performance Dashboard** — 49-day+ evidence summary

**Product positioning:** Analytics and decision support. Not gambling tips.
**Legal framing:** "VÉLØ provides race intelligence data for informational purposes. It does not constitute advice to bet."
