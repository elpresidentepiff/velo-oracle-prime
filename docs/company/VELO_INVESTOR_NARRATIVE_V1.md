## What VELO Is

VELO is an auditable racing intelligence operating system.

Most racing products sell picks with no traceable proof. VELO is building an intelligence layer that makes every important step inspectable:

- how the pick was made
- what confidence signals fired
- what the operator saw
- what happened after the race
- whether the system learned anything trustworthy

---

## Why This Matters

Racing is full of opinion and poor evidence hygiene.

VELO's edge is not just model output. It is operational truth:

- immutable evidence artifacts
- closed-result verification
- governed signal promotion
- visible risk flags
- clear operator interface

That combination is much harder to copy than a tips feed.
