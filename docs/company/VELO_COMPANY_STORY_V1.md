## The Short Version

VELO is an auditable racing intelligence operating system.

It does not just predict.  
It executes, records, audits, learns, verifies, and explains its own confidence.

---

## The Story

### First Era: Build VELO

The first era was about proving the machine could work at all:

- build the scoring engine
- produce live daily verdicts
- reconcile against closed results
- stop pretending and start recording

That produced the first body of truth.

### Second Era: Make VELO Legible

The second era is different. It asks:

- which signals inside VELO are actually trustworthy?
- how do we prove that to ourselves?
- how do we show it to the operator?
- how do we prevent promotion before the evidence is ready?

This is where VELO stops being a black-box prediction engine and becomes an auditable operating system.

---

## What VELO Has Learned About Itself

VELO now has repo-proven evidence that:

- VP30_TIER_A is a real trust boundary
- MDS_HIGH is a hidden upside signal
- IMPROVE_HIGH is a real quality signal
- B_LOW_VP_SUPPRESS is a drag warning
- the mid-price zone is the main miss battlefield

This means VELO is no longer just saying "pick this horse."

It can now say:

- why the pick is strong
- why the pick is weak
- which signal families fired
- what remains shadow-only
- what must not be promoted yet

That is a stronger company story because it is:
