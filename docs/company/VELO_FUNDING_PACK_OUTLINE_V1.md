We are pre-revenue and ready to build the product layer on top of a proven intelligence engine.

## What We Will NOT Claim

- That the system is profitable (no live staking has occurred)
- That router lanes are proven (shadow evidence only, n<30 on all lanes)
- That modifications caused performance improvements (correlation, not causality)
- That Playbook G V3 is production-ready (offline research only)
- That mid-priced winner conversion is solved (it is not)

Honesty is the product. Investors who want guaranteed returns should not fund us. Investors who want auditable, evidence-grade analytics in a large market should.
