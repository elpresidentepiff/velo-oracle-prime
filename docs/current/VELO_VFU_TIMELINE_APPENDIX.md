# APPENDIX: VFU-01 TO VFU-12 TIMELINE

The VÉLØ Forensics Unit (VFU) was established to move the system beyond simple outcome scoring into deep, governed behavioural analysis. The progression from VFU-01 to VFU-12 represents the establishment of a rigorous forensic investigation layer.

*   **VFU-01: Doctrine + schemas.** Established the foundational rules, database schemas, and operational boundaries for forensic investigation, ensuring a read-only, non-mutating start.
*   **VFU-02: 20-race autopsy dry-run.** A controlled test of the autopsy mechanism on a small, contained dataset to validate the extraction and classification of failure modes.
*   **VFU-03: pick_sp enrichment audit.** Focused on aligning price data with predictions to understand the market context of the selections.
*   **VFU-04: full current-era autopsy pass.** The first comprehensive sweep of recent data, categorizing misses and establishing the baseline error distribution.
*   **VFU-05: Pattern Prosecutor.** Introduced the mechanism to identify recurring themes in the autopsies, moving from isolated failures to structural weaknesses.
*   **VFU-06: Horse Identity Bridge.** A critical infrastructure update to link legacy name-based records with stable, immutable horse IDs, preserving namespaces.
*   **VFU-07: identity-confirmed repeated-horse review.** Leveraged VFU-06 to analyze horses that the engine repeatedly selected or repeatedly missed, exposing blind spots.
*   **VFU-08: Passport Review Queue.** Created the workflow for proposing updates to the canonical Horse Passport based on forensic evidence, locked behind a dry-run gate.
*   **VFU-09: VP Suppression Investigation.** A deep dive into whether a high Velo Probability (VP) was masking underlying weaknesses in specific profiles.
*   **VFU-10: Time-Safe Passport Override Validation.** The pivotal moment of temporal discipline. Investigated the 'Kakirra' anomaly, proving the signal was temporally contaminated and correctly rejecting its promotion.
*   **VFU-11: 6,019-row Sigma Master Ledger.** Assembled the definitive, era-separated dataset for structural investigation, quarantining older, potentially unsafe data.
*   **VFU-12: Sigma Pattern Tribunal + Human Review Triage.** Prosecuted 7 pattern candidates, generated a Top 25 human review queue, and promoted 3 specific patterns (VP suppression, SP shortening, Passport Override) to a dry-run watchlist only, enforcing the rule that no live doctrine is promoted without operator approval.
