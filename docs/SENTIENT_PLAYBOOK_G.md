# VÉLØ Sentient Playbook G
## The Loopback Architecture & Behavioral Doctrine

Sentient Playbook G is the self-reinforcing intelligence layer of the VÉLØ Oracle Prime system. It moves the platform beyond static modeling into a living, strategic organism that evolves based on its own performance.

### 1. Philosophical Definition: Self-Weaponization
Playbook G is built on the belief that a model is only as good as its last miss. Its purpose is to perform forensic attribution on every race to identify why the "Oracle" was deceived. It does not seek to be more accurate; it seeks to be more **profitable** by identifying when the market narrative is a trap.

### 2. The Multi-Agent Logic Loop
The engine operates on a continuous feedback loop:
`Observe → Classify → Compare → Imprint → Evolve → Redeploy`

Unlike standard machine learning which requires a retraining phase, Playbook G performs **imprint-learning** at the moment of reconciliation. If an A-Strike horse loses to a mid-priced plot, the system immediately updates its "Pain Genome" to recognize that specific structural setup as high-risk.

### 3. The 5 Evolution Pillars

#### Pillar 1: Behaviour Echo Chamber (BEC)
Logs "House" behavior. It tracks the relationship between market favorites and terminal outcomes to identify regimes of **Market Protection** (favorites winning) or **Market Abandonment** (outsiders winning).

#### Pillar 2: Structural Drift Engine (SDE)
Adaptive weighting for physical race dynamics.
- **Off-Pace Wins:** Adjusts if the track bias is favoring closers.
- **Draw SKEW:** Learns if specific stall positions are outperforming their historical baseline.
- **Hidden Improvers:** Identifies trainers who are successfully "hiding" form.

#### Pillar 3: Manipulation Memory Core (MMC)
The repository of market deception. The MMC identifies **Narrative Traps**—races where the "Spotlight" comment and market price align perfectly, yet the TopSpeed and OR compression data suggest an alternative reality.

#### Pillar 4: Recursive Emotion Engine (REE)
Translates results into "Machine Laws":
- **Pain Rules:** "Avoid [Setup X] when Market Volatility is high."
- **Triumph Rules:** "Trust the TopSpeed Engine during Class Drops."

#### Pillar 5: Appetite Multiplier (AM)
The ROI-driven aggression controller.
- **Winning/Profitable Streak:** AM increases aggression, loosening filters to capture high-value momentum.
- **Drawdown/Loss Sequence:** AM tightens criteria, retreating to a defensive floor (0.300) to protect the bankroll.

### 4. Unique Sub-Modules

#### The Kingmaker Module
A specialized logic gate that identifies the "Kingmaker"—the horse that didn't necessarily win, but caused the favorite to collapse. This allows VÉLØ to understand the **cause** of a race result, not just the result itself.

#### ROI-Driven Scaling
Playbook G is the first architecture to prioritize **Profit over Precision**. Standard models often optimize for hit rate; Playbook G optimizes for the **Aggression/ROI Delta**, meaning it would rather have a 10% strike rate at 20/1 than a 50% strike rate at 1/2.
