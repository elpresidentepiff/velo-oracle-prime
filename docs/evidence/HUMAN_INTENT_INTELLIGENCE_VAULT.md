## Rules Of Use

- Treat every entry as candidate intelligence only.
- Extract mechanisms, not tips.
- Separate observation from inference.
- Do not use these notes as live staking guidance.
- Do not connect any note to model fuel until it has forward evidence.

## Promotion Path

1. Raw transcript intake lands in this vault.
2. Mechanisms are classified into candidate features or operational doctrines.
3. Candidate mechanisms are tested against forward race evidence.
4. Only proven mechanisms can be promoted into runtime doctrine, Passport logic, or New Build scoring.
