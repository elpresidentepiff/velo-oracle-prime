# VÉLØ ORACLE PRIME
## An Applied Behavioural Prediction Engine

### 1. Executive Summary
VÉLØ Oracle Prime is an applied behavioural prediction engine, currently deployed in UK/Irish horse racing. It is built to decode intent, market distortion, horse identity, trainer/jockey behaviour, and probabilistic opportunity under live pressure. VÉLØ is not a gambling bot or a tipster service; it is a governed intelligence system designed to find structural truth in chaotic datasets. 

### 2. The Problem: Prediction Without Behaviour Is Blind
Most predictive models fail because they process historical data as if markets are rational and static. They ignore the human element: the unstated intent, the subtle shifts in market confidence, and the contextual pressures that shape outcomes. Prediction without understanding behaviour is merely statistical guessing.

### 3. Why Horse Racing Is the First Laboratory
Horse racing is not the product; horse racing is the live laboratory. It is chosen as the initial proving ground because it ruthlessly compresses behaviour, incentives, market movement, identity, timing, and outcome truth into measurable daily events. If an intelligence architecture can achieve predictive clarity in the brutal, high-noise environment of the betting ring, it possesses a core capability transferable to other complex domains.

### 4. What VÉLØ Is
VÉLØ is a complete behavioural prediction operating system. It observes a live market, generates probabilistic assessments, measures those assessments against market reality, and critically, conducts deep forensic autopsies on its failures to evolve its understanding of the underlying entities.

### 5. System Architecture
The VÉLØ architecture is built on a foundation of strict separation of concerns, ensuring that prediction, execution, and learning remain distinct and governable. The system operates on a daily intelligence loop, transitioning from morning data capture to pre-race evaluation, and finally to evening forensic reconciliation.

### 6. Prediction Engine
VÉLØ began as a horse-racing prediction engine but has evolved into a behavioural prediction OS. Its core components include SQPE / VÉLØ Prime scoring, market-deception awareness, course strength profiling, and false-GREEN anomaly detection. The engine does not just look for the fastest horse; it looks for the highest convergence of intent and capability.

### 7. VP Gatekeeper
VP (Velo Probability) serves as a permission signal, a confidence filter, and a daily opportunity classifier. It is not yet a live scoring formula or a staking automation trigger. Current-era dry-run evidence demonstrates that VP >= 0.40 shows a strong strike-rate uplift versus the baseline. However, VP remains a population signal, not a hard individual-horse disqualifier. Recent false-GREEN anomalies have proven the necessity of maintaining caution and robust day-level anomaly detection.

### 8. Horse Passport Memory Layer
The Horse Passport is the canonical living dossier of the horse. While official form tells us what happened, the VÉLØ Horse Passport tries to understand what the horse is *becoming*. Crucially, the Passport operates under strict governance: it is canonical, it does not mutate automatically based on model failures, and all updates require explicit operator approval following a time-safe audit.

### 9. VÉLØ Forensics Unit
The VÉLØ Forensics Unit (VFU) is the breakthrough layer of the architecture. VFU does not merely ask whether VÉLØ won or lost. It asks: What did VÉLØ say before the race? Why did it say it? What happened? Was the logic right? Was the result unlucky? Was the failure due to VP, course, identity, price, data quality, or time contamination? Sigma learns patterns. Playbook G trains strategy. VP Gatekeeper controls engagement. VÉLØ Forensics investigates truth. Horse Passport remembers the living horse.

### 10. Pattern Prosecutor and Tribunal
The Pattern Prosecutor identifies recurring behavioural anomalies across the autopsy data. The VFU Tribunal then prosecutes these patterns. In the recent VFU-12 Tribunal, 7 pattern candidates were examined, leading to the promotion of 3 (VP suppression, SP shortening, and Passport Override) to a dry-run watchlist only.

### 11. Governance, Safety, and No-Drift Architecture
VÉLØ operates behind a hard safety perimeter. There is no autonomous model promotion, no live scoring mutation without authorization, and no automated writes to the canonical Supabase datastore during forensic tasks. The repository mandates governed CI execution, ensuring that the architecture cannot drift into unproven or unsafe states. There is strictly no live doctrine promotion without explicit operator approval.

### 12. Evidence So Far
The system has completed a rigorous forensic escalation from VFU-01 through VFU-12. This process generated a 6,019-row Sigma Master Ledger spanning 5 distinct data sources. 3,052 rows have been current-era validated, while older, structurally distinct data (2,165 rows) remains under strict quarantine.

### 13. What the Evidence Proves
The current-era validation proves that the system can autonomously identify and categorize complex failure modes. It proves that signals like VP >= 0.40 correlate with performance uplift in dry-run scenarios. It proves the system possesses the structural capacity for deep behavioural decoding.

### 14. What the Evidence Does Not Yet Prove
The evidence does not prove a guaranteed ROI. SP (Starting Price) and ROI coverage remain limited in the current validated sets. The system does not claim production readiness for autonomous live staking based on newly discovered patterns.

### 15. Data Quality and Time-Safety Discipline
"No evidence becomes doctrine unless it was knowable before the race." This is a critical trust point. The investigation into the horse 'Kakirra' appeared to reveal a major Passport-over-VP discovery. However, VFU-10 proved the Passport evidence was temporally contaminated. Consequently, Kakirra was rejected as predictive proof. This demonstrates the system's capacity for self-correction and its refusal to promote false doctrine.

### 16. Market Opportunity
The immediate market is the application of advanced predictive analytics within the racing sector. However, the true value lies in the intellectual property of the architecture itself—a system capable of isolating signal from extreme noise in a live, adversarial environment.

### 17. Why This Can Expand Beyond Racing
Most AI prediction systems fail because they only optimise outputs. VÉLØ is building an evidence-governed behavioural intelligence loop (prediction → result → autopsy → identity memory → pattern prosecution → time-safety audit → dry-run promotion → operator approval). This repeatable architecture for behavioural prediction is highly applicable to markets where incentives, timing, and hidden intent matter, such as sports analytics, betting exchanges, financial market microstructure, insurance risk, fraud detection, and talent scouting.

### 20. Investor Thesis
